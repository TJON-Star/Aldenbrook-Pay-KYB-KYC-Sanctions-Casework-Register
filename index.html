2. Screening decision flow (sanctions / PEP)

The test passes only if multiple identifiers are inconsistent, the gap is too large to be a data-entry error, and at least one identifier is corroborated by a document. A single small discrepancy, or a listed record that is a currently active high-risk role, escalates.
